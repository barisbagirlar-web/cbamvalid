## Ready-to-send appeal

Hello Paddle Domain Review Team,

I am appealing the rejection of `cbamvalid.com` under the category `Other/Government Services` because the product is not a government service.

CBAMValid is a privately operated, self-service B2B software application owned and operated by SectorCalc Corporation. The customer enters and controls its own business data. The application then performs deterministic calculations, automated quality controls, customer-controlled evidence linking, and automated PDF, JSON and XLSX generation.

The commercial product is a one-time USD 449 software unlock for one customer-controlled working file covering one operator, one installation and one reporting year. Digital files are generated automatically by the software after a successful lock.

The purchase does not include or provide:

- government services or representation before a public authority;
- registry filing, customs filing, permit applications or submissions on a customer’s behalf;
- access to government officials or preferential authority treatment;
- consulting, advisory services, managed compliance or manual dossier preparation;
- legal, tax or customs advice;
- accredited verification, certification, approval or assurance opinions;
- human review or approval of customer emissions data.

CBAM and EU regulatory references appear because the software implements published calculation rules and documents the ruleset used. These references are calculation and documentation inputs inside a software product. CBAMValid is not an EU institution, customs authority, registry submission service, government contractor or accredited verifier.

We have revised the public website so the software classification and excluded services are explicit on the homepage, pricing page, terms and a dedicated product-classification page:

- `https://cbamvalid.com/`
- `https://cbamvalid.com/pricing`
- `https://cbamvalid.com/product-classification`
- `https://cbamvalid.com/terms`

A public software walkthrough and sample automated digital outputs are available here:

- `https://cbamvalid.com/demo`
- `https://cbamvalid.com/sample-dossier`

Please manually re-review `cbamvalid.com` as B2B SaaS / self-service software with automated digital delivery rather than Government Services. We can provide a test account and any additional product ownership or processing information you require.
